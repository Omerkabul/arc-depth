arc-depth

Which of this Arc token’s pools can you actually trade through, and what does a buy-then-sell round trip cost? Read-only — no wallet, no signing, no transactions.

Size is in USDC. Both legs are quoted at one pinned block; nothing is sent to the chain.

What this does not claim
  • Unmeasured is not unsafe. A failed quote is reported as such, never as “cannot sell”.
  • Uniswap v4 only. Arc also has v3 pools; the v4 quoter cannot price them.
  • Dynamic-fee pools are flagged, not priced — the hook sets the fee at swap time.
  • One block only. Hooks and liquidity can change in the next one.
  • The public Arc RPC is rate limited; rapid repeat requests degrade.
